Main traded products

Top five HS4 headings by share of exports and imports, from UN Comtrade. Needs COMTRADE_API_KEY on the server. The products follow the trade figures: after every trade sync (weekly, and at start-up) each economy is re-pulled only when Comtrade has published a newer year than the one stored; economies already current cost no calls. One call per economy and flow against the daily Comtrade budget shared with Export Potential; a run stopped by the budget resumes next time.
